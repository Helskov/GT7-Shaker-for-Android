Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the GT7 Shaker for Android mobile application. Our commitment to your privacy is simple: we do not collect your data.

1. Data Collection and Usage

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or information. The app does not contain any input fields, forms, or prompts that require you to enter personal information (such as names, emails, or phone numbers).

2. Local Network Operation

The app operates exclusively on your Local Area Network (LAN) to facilitate communication between your Android device and your PlayStation console. No data from this local communication is ever sent to external servers or third-party entities.

3. User Accounts

The app does not require a user account, registration, or any form of login process.

4. Third-Party Services

We do not use any third-party analytics, tracking tools, or advertising networks. Your activity within the app remains entirely private and local to your device.

5. Children's Privacy

Since the app does not collect any personal information, it is inherently safe for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect or request data from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
